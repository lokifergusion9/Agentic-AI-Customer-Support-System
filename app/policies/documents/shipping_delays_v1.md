## Section 1: Scope

This policy covers questions about where an order is and orders that arrive later than expected.
Returns of delivered items are covered by the Return Processing Policy.

## Section 2: Estimated delivery date

Every order is given an estimated delivery date at checkout. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7
business days from dispatch, depending on the delivery address. The estimated delivery date shown
to the customer is the only delivery date the store commits to.

## Section 3: Shipment statuses

The courier reports one of the following shipment statuses:

- **label_created:** the order is packed and waiting for the courier to collect it
- **in_transit:** the courier has the parcel and it is moving through the network
- **out_for_delivery:** the parcel is with the delivery agent for delivery today
- **delivered:** the courier has recorded the parcel as delivered
- **exception:** the courier has reported a problem, such as a failed delivery attempt or a
  damaged label

## Section 4: When an order counts as delayed

An order is delayed when it has not been delivered by its estimated delivery date. A delay of up
to 3 business days is a minor delay. Support shares the latest shipment status with the customer
and does not offer compensation.

A delay of more than 3 business days, or any shipment with the status exception, is a significant
delay. Support shares the latest status and refers the case to a human reviewer, who may contact
the courier.

If an order is more than 7 business days past its estimated delivery date and has not been
delivered, it is treated as possibly lost and referred to a human reviewer to trace the shipment
or arrange a resolution.

## Section 5: Communicating shipment status

Support tells the customer the latest shipment status and when the courier last updated it, using
only the information recorded by the courier. Support does not give a new delivery date unless the
courier has provided one. If the shipment status cannot be retrieved, support tells the customer
that the status is not available right now, and does not guess.

## Section 6: Delivered but not received

If the courier shows the order as delivered but the customer says it has not arrived, the case is
referred to a human reviewer. Support does not offer a refund or replacement for these cases.
