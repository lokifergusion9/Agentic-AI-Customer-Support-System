## Section 1: Scope

This policy covers items that arrive damaged, broken or not working. Damage caused after delivery
is not covered. Returns are handled under the Return Processing Policy, and refunds under the
Refund Eligibility Policy.

## Section 2: Reporting window

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of the delivery date. Reports made after 48 hours are
referred to a human reviewer and are not resolved under this policy.

## Section 3: Required information

Before a resolution is prepared, support collects all of the following:

- the order the item belongs to
- a short description of the damage or fault
- at least one photo of the damaged item
- a photo of the outer packaging as delivered
- the serial number of the item, where the item has one

If any of this is missing, support asks the customer for the missing items only and does not
prepare a resolution until they are provided.

## Section 4: Resolution options

When the required information has been collected, the customer is offered one of the following:

- **Replacement:** the same item is sent once the damaged item has been collected, if it is in
  stock. A replacement is arranged by a support reviewer. Support can propose it but does not
  place the replacement order itself.
- **Refund:** if a replacement is not in stock or the customer prefers a refund, the damaged item
  is returned and refunded under the Refund Eligibility Policy. The refund needs approval from a
  support reviewer, as every refund does.

For a damaged item, inspection passes when the warehouse confirms that the reported damage was
present at delivery. Because the damage was a store or courier error, shipping charges are also
refunded.

## Section 5: Cases referred to a human reviewer

The following cases are referred to a human reviewer instead of being resolved directly:

- the item is a safety risk, such as a swollen battery, smoke, burning smell or exposed wiring
- the photos do not show damage that matches the description
- the customer has reported damage on more than one recent order
- the damage was reported after the 48-hour window

For a safety risk, support first tells the customer to stop using the item and keep it unplugged.

## Section 6: Communicating with the customer

Support does not promise a replacement or a refund before the required information has been
collected and a resolution has been prepared. Support does not decide whether a claim is
fraudulent. Suspected fraud is referred to a human reviewer.
