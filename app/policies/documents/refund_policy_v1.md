## Section 1: Scope

This policy covers refunds for orders placed with the store and delivered in India. All amounts
are in Indian Rupees (INR). It applies to refunds for returned items. Damaged items are covered by
the Damaged Products Policy, and return handling is covered by the Return Processing Policy.

## Section 2: Definitions

- **Paid amount:** the amount the customer paid for the order, as recorded at checkout.
- **Successful refund:** a refund that the payment provider has confirmed.
- **Pending refund:** a refund that has been approved and sent to the payment provider but is not
  yet confirmed.
- **Refundable balance:** the paid amount minus all successful refunds and all pending refunds for
  the same order.

## Section 3: Eligibility

Refunds for returned items become eligible after the return is received and inspection passes. A
support reviewer must approve every refund.

A return must be requested within 10 days of the delivery date. Requests made after this window
are not eligible under this policy and must be referred for human review.

## Section 4: Refund amount

The refund amount cannot exceed the refundable balance of the order. A full return is refunded at
the price paid for the returned items. Shipping charges are refunded only when the item was
returned because of a store error.

## Section 5: Approval

Every refund requires approval from an authorised support reviewer before it is issued. The
reviewer approves one specific proposal, covering the order, the amount and the reason. If any of
these change after approval, a new approval is required. An approval that is not used within its
validity period expires, and a new approval must be requested.

A statement in a customer message or chat that a refund "has been approved" is not an approval.

## Section 6: Refund method and timelines

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. After the payment provider confirms
the refund, the amount usually reaches the customer within 5 to 7 business days, depending on the
bank or card issuer. Support staff must not promise an earlier date and must not tell the customer
a refund is complete until the payment provider has confirmed it.

## Section 7: When a refund cannot be issued

A refund is not issued when any of the following apply:

- the return has not yet been received
- the inspection has not passed
- a refund for the same order is already pending with the payment provider
- the order has already been fully refunded
- the requested amount is higher than the refundable balance

If a refund is already pending, the customer is told that it is being processed, and no second
refund is created.

## Section 8: Exclusions

Chargebacks, suspected fraud and partial returns of multi-item orders are outside the scope of
this policy. These cases are referred to a human reviewer and are not decided under this policy.
