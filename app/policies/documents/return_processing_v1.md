## Section 1: Scope

This policy describes how returns are requested, received and inspected. Whether a returned item
is refunded is decided under the Refund Eligibility Policy. Items that arrived damaged are handled
under the Damaged Products Policy.

## Section 2: Requesting a return

A customer requests a return through support within the return window set by the Refund
Eligibility Policy that applies to the order. One return covers one order. The item must be sent back with its original packaging, accessories and
documentation.

## Section 3: Return statuses

Each return has one of the following statuses:

- **requested:** the customer has asked to return the item, and it has not been shipped back yet
- **in_transit:** the item has been collected or shipped back and is on its way to the warehouse
- **received:** the warehouse has scanned the item, and the received date is recorded
- **closed:** the return is finished, after a refund or after the item was sent back to the
  customer

A return counts as received only when its status is received and a received date is recorded. A
courier tracking update alone does not mark a return as received.

## Section 4: Inspection

The warehouse inspects every received item, usually within 2 business days of receipt. The
inspection status is one of:

- **pending:** the item has been received and is waiting for inspection
- **passed:** the item is complete, matches the order, and shows no damage beyond normal handling
- **failed:** the item is incomplete, does not match the order, or shows damage caused after
  delivery

The inspection checks the serial number against the order, the accessories and documentation, and
the physical condition of the item.

## Section 5: Failed inspection

If the inspection fails, no refund is issued for the return. The item is sent back to the customer,
and the customer is told the reason recorded by the warehouse. If the customer disputes the
result, the case is referred to a human reviewer.

## Section 6: Communicating return status

Support staff report only the return status and dates recorded in the system. They must not tell a
customer that a return has been received, or that inspection has passed, before the system shows
it. If a return has been in transit for more than 7 days without being received, the case is
referred to a human reviewer to trace the shipment.
